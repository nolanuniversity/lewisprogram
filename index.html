Programme Philosophy

Understand Finance. Master Investment. Manage Capital. Create Financial Value.

"Finance is not only about understanding numbers. It is about understanding value, assessing risk and making informed decisions about capital."

The Bachelor of Finance & Investment is a rigorous three-year online degree designed to develop professionals with a deep understanding of finance, investment and capital markets.

The programme provides students with a comprehensive foundation in financial analysis, economics, accounting, corporate finance, investment analysis, financial markets, portfolio management, banking, valuation, risk management, wealth management and global finance.

Students progress from understanding the fundamentals of finance to analysing investments and ultimately managing capital and making sophisticated financial decisions.

PRINCIPLE 01

Understand Finance

Develop a strong understanding of financial systems, accounting, economics, financial institutions, markets and the principles that govern financial decision-making.

PRINCIPLE 02

Master Investment

Learn how to analyse securities, evaluate investment opportunities, value companies, construct portfolios and assess risk and return.

PRINCIPLE 03

Manage Capital

Understand how individuals, businesses and institutions allocate, finance, invest and manage capital.

PRINCIPLE 04

Create Financial Value

Develop the analytical judgement required to make responsible financial and investment decisions that support sustainable long-term value.

Year 1 Applied Project

Financial Statement & Company Analysis Project

Students select a company and undertake a structured financial analysis — examining revenue and profitability, assets and liabilities, cash flows, financial ratios, capital structure, financial performance, industry conditions and financial risks. The final output is a professional Company Financial Analysis Report, presented by the student.

Move Into Investment & Financial Analysis

Year 2 moves students into more advanced financial and investment analysis. Students develop the ability to evaluate investments, value financial assets, analyse companies, construct portfolios and assess financial risk.

Year 2 Applied Project

Investment Analysis & Portfolio Project

Students develop and manage a simulated investment portfolio — establishing investment objectives, researching securities, analysing companies, conducting investment valuation, assessing risk and expected return, constructing a diversified portfolio, developing an investment strategy, monitoring investment performance and evaluating their decisions. Final submission: Investment Research Report + Portfolio Report + Investment Presentation.

Become a Capital & Investment Manager

The final year develops advanced financial judgement and prepares students for professional environments across finance and investment. Students move from analysing individual financial decisions to understanding the management of capital, portfolios, institutions and wealth.

Year 3 Capstone

Lewis Finance & Investment Capstone — Three Pathways

The culminating academic and professional experience of the degree. Students undertake a substantial finance or investment project demonstrating their ability to integrate knowledge from across the programme. Choose one of three finance-focused pathways — Investment Management, Corporate Finance & Valuation, or Wealth & Investment Management.

Year 3 Capstone

Three Pathways. One Substantial Outcome.

Every student leaves with a professionally presented finance or investment deliverable — not just a transcript.

PATHWAY 01

Investment Management

Develop and defend an investment strategy and portfolio — including investment objectives, asset allocation, security selection, portfolio construction, risk analysis, performance evaluation and investment recommendations.

PATHWAY 02

Corporate Finance & Valuation

Conduct a comprehensive corporate finance and valuation analysis — including financial forecasting, company valuation, capital structure, cost of capital, capital budgeting, transaction analysis and corporate financial strategy.

PATHWAY 03

Wealth & Investment Management

Develop a comprehensive investment strategy for a hypothetical individual, family or private client — including financial objectives, investment objectives, risk assessment, asset allocation, portfolio construction, wealth preservation and long-term investment strategy.
